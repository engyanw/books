However, stress is not always a bad thing. A certain amount of stress can push us to work harder and achieve our goals. The problem arises when stress becomes too much to handle.

To deal with stress, the first step is to find out where it comes from. Is it a difficult subject like math? Or is it a relationship problem with a friend? Once you identify the source, you can take action. For example, if math is the problem, you can ask a teacher for help or join a study group.

Second, take time to relax. Many students think they should study 24 hours a day, but this is actually counterproductive (适得其反). The brain needs rest to function well. Doing sports, listening to music, or just taking a short walk can help clear your mind.

Third, talk to someone. Don't keep your feelings inside. Share your worries with your parents, teachers, or friends. Sometimes, just talking about your problems can make you feel much better. They might also offer you useful advice.

Finally, keep a positive attitude. Accept that nobody is perfect. It is okay to make mistakes. Learn from them and move on. Remember, high school is not just about getting good grades; it is also about growing up and learning how to face challenges.
